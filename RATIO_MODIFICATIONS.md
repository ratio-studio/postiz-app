# Ratio Postiz customization

This fork contains Ratio-specific modifications to Postiz, based on the upstream repository at https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app.

Modified on 2026-09-25:
- Reduced YouTube OAuth scopes to the scopes required by the provider implementation.
- Replaced the most visible Postiz logos, wordmarks, preview branding, and theme accents with Ratio branding.
- Added a dedicated GHCR workflow for ghcr.io/ratio-studio/postiz-app:ratio.

The upstream AGPL-3.0 license and notices remain in place.
